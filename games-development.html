3D Survival Horror Prototype — Honours Dissertation

Solo · dissertation

A research-driven horror prototype exploring how different cue types (audio, lighting, visual prompts) affect player behaviour and perception, built entirely solo across every discipline: programming, AI, animation, sound, and 3D modelling.

Unreal EngineBlueprintsBlender
  • Owned

    Every discipline solo for a complete playable prototype: programming, enemy AI, character controls, animation, sound design, and 3D modelling in Blender, not just one slice of a team project.

  • Built

    Enemy AI, character controls, and the horror-specific audio and lighting cue systems the research itself was testing, programmed in Unreal Blueprints.

  • Researched

    Planned and ran user testing sessions, then documented methodology and findings in a full dissertation, real UX research discipline behind the build, not just a shipped prototype.
